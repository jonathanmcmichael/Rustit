## Open review areas

Rustit is actively seeking contributors who can grow into reviewers for:

- BIM authoring and VDC workflows;
- project controls, CPM, and 4D planning;
- IFC 4.3 semantics and round trips;
- CAD kernels, topology, tolerances, and graphics;
- PostgreSQL/PostGIS, transactions, and collaboration;
- vendor schedule integrations;
- security and supply-chain engineering; and
- contributor experience and technical writing.

Review work is a first-class contribution. See `GOVERNANCE.md` for how roles grow.
