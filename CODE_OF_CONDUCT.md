# Code of Conduct

Rustit exists to combine knowledge from construction, design, software, scheduling, standards, and open-source communities. Productive disagreement is welcome; contempt, harassment, and gatekeeping are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, respectful, and generous about differing backgrounds.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people or their choice of tools.
- Make room for domain experts who are new to Rust and developers who are new to construction.
- State uncertainty, correct mistakes openly, and preserve useful failed experiments.
- Protect customer data, credentials, licensed material, and private reports.
- Avoid sexualized language, personal attacks, intimidation, discriminatory conduct, and sustained disruption.

These expectations apply in repository activity and in public spaces where someone is representing Rustit.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the bootstrap maintainer using the contact method on [@jonathanmcmichael's GitHub profile](https://github.com/jonathanmcmichael). Do not expose sensitive details in a public issue. If the maintainer is involved in the concern, use GitHub's abuse-reporting channel until Rustit has an independent second maintainer.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, privately warn participants, temporarily limit participation, or permanently remove contributors from project spaces. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately, and mindful of reporter safety.

## Attribution

This project-specific policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and may adopt a fuller community enforcement process as the maintainer group grows.
